How to Choose a Coffee Blend

By Anna K. · March 2024

Choosing the right coffee blend can feel overwhelming with so many options available. Whether you prefer a light and fruity cup or a dark and bold espresso, understanding the basics of coffee blending will help you find your perfect match. Single-origin coffees offer a unique taste tied to their region, while blends combine beans from different origins to create a balanced and consistent flavor. Neither is better — it all depends on what you enjoy.
